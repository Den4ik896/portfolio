Frequently asked questions

Can I pay by card?

Yes, we accept cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. You pay when you pick up your order.

Do you have plant-based milk?

Oat, almond and coconut — at no extra charge for any drink.

Do you sell beans to take home?

Yes, in 250 g and 1 kg bags. We grind them for your brew method for free.

Can I work from the café?

Of course. We have fast Wi-Fi and a power outlet at every table.
