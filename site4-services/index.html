    Questions & answers

    Everything you need to know before booking.

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Many customers leave a key or door code. You get a message when the cleaner arrives and when the job is finished.

    Do cleaners bring their own supplies?

    Yes — all products and equipment are included. If you prefer your own products, just tell us in the booking notes.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.

    What if I am not satisfied?

    Tell us within 24 hours and we will send a cleaner back to fix it at no cost.
